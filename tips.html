🚗 GTA: Driving & Exploring

Driving like a pro

  • Ease off the gas before a corner, then speed up on the way out. You'll be quicker than if you slam the brakes halfway through.
  • Every car handles differently. Sports cars grip well, while big trucks and vans are slow to turn. Try a few and find a favourite.
  • Motorbikes are fast, but if you hit something you can fall off. Take it easy until you get the hang of them.
  • Try different camera views (including first-person) and use whichever one helps you steer best.

Racing

  • Watch the checkpoints and the map. Knowing where the next turn is matters more than top speed.
  • Take the smooth "racing line": go wide into a corner, cut to the inside, then drift wide again on the way out.
  • Crashed? Don't panic. Get straight back on track. Staying calm wins races.

Customising cars

  • At the mod shop you can change paint, wheels and more. Upgrades like engine, brakes and tyres make a car faster and grippier.
  • Upgrade the car you drive most. That's the best use of your in-game money.

Exploring & missions

  • The map is huge. Try a road trip to the mountains, the beach or the countryside just to see the views.
  • Stuck on a mission? Read the on-screen instructions carefully and check the map markers, then try a different approach.
  • Save your game often so you don't lose progress.

🧱 Roblox

Stay safe online 🛡️

  • Never share your password, full name, school, address or phone number, even with "friends" in a game.
  • Nobody legit will ever ask for your password. Anyone offering "free Robux" is trying to scam you.
  • If someone is mean or makes you feel weird, block and report them, then tell Dad.
  • Only buy things with Robux from the official Roblox store or inside the game itself.

Obby tips

  • Line up your jump first. Moving the camera to look down at your feet helps a lot.
  • On tricky jumps, run and jump right at the edge of the platform.
  • Slow and steady beats rushing. Every checkpoint you reach is progress!

Building in Roblox Studio 🛠️

  • Roblox Studio is free and lets you make your own games.
  • Start simple, with parts like blocks, wedges and spheres. Use the Move, Scale and Rotate tools to shape them.
  • "Anchor" parts that shouldn't fall down, otherwise they'll drop or tumble when you press Play.
  • Press Play to test often. Building a small obby first is a great starter project.
  • Want to go further? Roblox games are coded in a language called Lua (Luau). Try a script that changes a part's colour.

🏗️ Fortnite

Building & editing

  • The four basic builds are wall, floor, stairs (ramp) and roof (cone). Practise switching between them fast.
  • Wood builds fastest but is weakest. Brick is in the middle. Metal is slowest to build but toughest once finished.
  • Editing lets you add windows, doors and arches to your builds. Practise edits until they feel quick.
  • Creative mode is perfect for practising building and editing with no pressure.

Smart strategy

  • Watch the storm timer on your map and start moving toward the safe zone early. Rushing at the last second is risky.
  • Collect materials (wood, brick, metal) along the way so you're ready to build.
  • High ground gives you a great view of what's around you.
  • Playing with friends? Talk to your squad and share supplies. Teamwork wins!

Creative mode fun 🎨

  • Build your own island: a race track, a parkour course or a basketball court!
  • Try other players' parkour and deathrun maps to get better at movement.

Even pro gamers take breaks! After a good session, rest your eyes, grab some water, and maybe shoot a few hoops 🏀 or read a chapter 📚. Your brain comes back sharper and you earn scoreboard points. Double win! 🙌
